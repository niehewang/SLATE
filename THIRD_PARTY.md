# Third-party components

SLATE wraps or evaluates external evidence/threat baselines. The public repository should not imply authorship of those upstream methods.

## LLMPrint
Used in the P2 threat characterization chain. This repository contains SLATE-side orchestration/adaptation scripts and frozen metadata, not a claim of ownership over LLMPrint itself.

## LLM-DNA / RepTrace
Used as a structurally meaningful lineage evidence engine in P3/P5/P6/P7. The frozen experiment referenced the official LLM-DNA/RepTrace 1.0.1 release/commit recorded by the experiment manifests. Upstream source and license obligations remain with that project.

## Model and dataset assets
Qwen, Phi, sentence-transformer/Qwen embedding checkpoints, WildChat/ShareGPT/OASST-derived assets, and any other public data/model artifacts are not redistributed here. Obtain them from their respective upstream sources or approved mirrors and comply with their licenses/terms.
